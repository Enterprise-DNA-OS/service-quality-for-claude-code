One seat is US$420 annually. A 50-agent scenario is US$21,000 annually for the add-on alone. This is arithmetic from the public rate, not an observed invoice, a quote or evidence that an existing Enterprise DNA client pays it. The comparison page quotes only the single-seat price and its annual equivalent.

Repository searches did not verify named NZ/AU customer use; no live customer system was accessed. Search results show comparison pages and alternatives, not measured search volume. [Zendesk's comparison article](https://www.zendesk.com/service/quality-assurance/quality-management-software/) names EvaluAgent and Scorebuddy among alternatives. Do not convert these observations into search-volume claims or invented customer complaints.

## Product rituals

[Scorecards](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8875998154906-Viewing-and-managing-scorecards) define evaluation categories and scales. [Calibration](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/7043724530842-Setting-up-calibration-in-Zendesk-QA) compares reviewers on the same conversation and keeps calibration out of normal quality scores. The [admin guide](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10093676975898-Getting-started-with-Zendesk-QA-Admin-guide) describes recurring reviews, monitoring, coaching and calibration. These shaped the local commands. The base is human-reviewed and does not claim Zendesk's automated analysis of every conversation.

[Native export documentation](https://developer.zendesk.com/documentation/qa/exporting-data/) specifies saved JSON containing conversations, review identities, category ratings, scores and comments. The import fixture is fictional data in that documented shape, not a captured customer account. Historical vendor scores remain separate from the local weighted score. Voice transcripts and dashboard metrics are excluded by the vendor export.

Legal sources and operational limits live in compliance.md. All demo people, conversations and evidence are fictional. The comparison page's three examples are captured from real command runs on those fictional records.
