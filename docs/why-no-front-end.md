# Why this quality desk has no front end

The recurring work is a conversation register, assessment standards, criterion ratings, reviewer calibration, coaching commitments and appeal decisions. An operator can describe those jobs to a coding agent, read the actual records and write the approved change through one CLI.

The free version produces read-only HTML reports and branded paperwork. It does not provide staff logins, a mobile reviewer app, offline audio playback, drag-and-drop assessment forms or a live feed of helpdesk tickets. Source recordings and ticket handling remain in their existing systems. This makes the base useful for the manual quality desk without claiming to replace every Zendesk feature.

A custom version can add the team's screens, authenticated access and helpdesk connections. Those are scoped delivery work. The database and scoring rules remain inspectable and portable.

Change brand.json for your business name, logo and colours. /new-view adds another report. /customise changes the fields or rules through a migration and tests. The CLI works with Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor through the same instructions.
